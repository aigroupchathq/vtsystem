# 02 — Brand Identity & Visual Assets: VEDIC TREE OS

## 1. Brand Essence & Cultural Roots

The VEDIC TREE brand represents growth, ancient wisdom (the Banyan tree, Vedic heritage), and forward-looking digital empowerment.

---

## 2. Logo System & Clearspace Rules

### 2.1 Logo Variants
1. **Primary Logotype**: The stylized geometric Vedic Tree emblem paired with modern geometric wordmark "VEDIC TREE OS". Used on marketing headers, login screens, and executive decks.
2. **Compact Mark / Favicon**: The isolated Tree emblem inside a soft-cornered square. Used in collapsed sidebars, mobile headers, browser favicons, and PWA launch icons.
3. **School Co-Branding Lockup**: Standardized horizontal lockup pairing the Vedic Tree OS mark with the individual school/franchise logo and campus name:
   `[VT OS Mark] | [Vedic Tree International School, Baner]`

### 2.2 Clearspace & Minimum Dimensions
- **Clearspace**: Maintain minimum padding equal to `0.5x` the height of the tree emblem around all sides of the logo.
- **Minimum Digital Sizes**:
  - Full Logotype: Minimum width `140px` (or `32px` height).
  - Emblem Mark: Minimum `24 x 24px`.

---

## 3. Brand Imagery & Cultural Motifs

1. **Subtle Background Geometry**: Subtle mathematical sacred geometry (fractals, mandala grids, golden ratios) at ultra-low opacity (2-3%) may be used on login canvases and welcome screens.
2. **Photography Standards**: Real, authentic photos of Indian and international classrooms, active students, sports, science labs, and teacher interactions. Avoid stock photography with generic Western classroom aesthetics or artificial staged poses.
