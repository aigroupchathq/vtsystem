# 07 — Role-Based Experiences & Permissions: VEDIC TREE OS

## 1. Principles of Role-Specific UX

VEDIC TREE OS does not present a single generic interface with hidden buttons. Each of the 11 key roles receives a **tailored operational cockpit** optimized specifically for their daily rhythm, cognitive load, and primary devices.

---

## 2. Detailed Role Profiles & Workspaces

### 2.1 HQ Executive & Board of Directors
- **Primary Viewport**: Desktop / Large TV Displays / Executive Tablets.
- **Cognitive Goal**: Global macro-oversight, trend analysis, compliance health, revenue pacing.
- **Key Modules Visible**:
  - Network Enrollment & Capacity Utilization across all regions.
  - Consolidated Fee Collection vs. Overdue Accounts.
  - Franchise Royalty Reconciliation & Contract Compliance.
  - Standardized Academic Board Performance Benchmarks.
- **Tone & Interaction**: High data density, sparklines, comparative geographical maps, exportable executive board decks.

### 2.2 School Principal
- **Primary Viewport**: Laptop / iPad / Smartphone during campus walkabouts.
- **Cognitive Goal**: Total operational command of their specific school campus.
- **Key Modules Visible**:
  - Morning Readiness: Staff attendance, unassigned teacher substitutions.
  - Student Safety & Transport: Bus departure statuses, emergency gate alerts.
  - Academic Health: Syllabus pacing bottlenecks, upcoming board examinations.
  - Disciplinary & Parent Escalations: Critical tickets open > 24 hours.
- **Tone & Interaction**: Exception-driven alerts, quick approval buttons, voice-note attachments.

### 2.3 Head of Department (HOD)
- **Primary Viewport**: Laptop in departmental staff room.
- **Cognitive Goal**: Academic standards, teacher mentoring, syllabus coverage, exam moderation.
- **Key Modules Visible**:
  - Curriculum progress tracker across all subject divisions.
  - Pending lesson plan approvals submitted by teachers.
  - Marks moderation queue for mid-term and final examinations.
- **Tone & Interaction**: Side-by-side comparison tables, diff view of lesson plan revisions, inline review comments.

### 2.4 Classroom & Subject Teacher
- **Primary Viewport**: Budget Android smartphone or shared classroom tablet.
- **Cognitive Goal**: Zero distraction from students, rapid classroom administration.
- **Key Modules Visible**:
  - Today's Class Schedule with current period highlighted.
  - Quick Attendance: Big touch targets, one-tap absentee marking.
  - Digital Homework & Class Diary publisher with camera capture for handwritten board notes.
  - Gradebook entry with keyboard numerical keypad optimization.
- **Tone & Interaction**: High contrast, tactile tap feedback, minimal typing required, offline capability.

### 2.5 School Administrator / Registrar
- **Primary Viewport**: Desktop with full keyboard and mouse.
- **Cognitive Goal**: High-throughput records management, compliance, official documentation.
- **Key Modules Visible**:
  - Student Master Directory with advanced multi-field filtering.
  - Admissions document verification and batch approval.
  - Transfer Certificate (TC), Bonafide, and ID Card batch generation.
- **Tone & Interaction**: Compact data tables, keyboard shortcuts, multi-row selection, CSV batch imports.

### 2.6 Finance & Cashier
- **Primary Viewport**: Desktop with dual displays and thermal receipt printer.
- **Cognitive Goal**: Zero cash discrepancy, rapid queue clearance, instant reconciliation.
- **Key Modules Visible**:
  - Cashier POS Desk with instant search by roll/admission/phone number.
  - Dynamic UPI QR generation for customer-facing display.
  - Automated late fee calculation and authorized discount/concession overrides.
  - End-of-day cash drawer balancing and Bank Deposit slip generation.
- **Tone & Interaction**: Financial-grade clarity, clear currency formatting (₹ / $), no ambiguous states.

### 2.7 HR & Payroll Manager
- **Primary Viewport**: Desktop.
- **Cognitive Goal**: Biometric hardware synchronization, compliant leave calculation, punctual salary processing.
- **Key Modules Visible**:
  - Daily Biometric device sync health and exception logs.
  - Leave ledger and approval workflows.
  - Monthly payroll computation, statutory deduction engine (PF/ESI/TDS in India).
- **Tone & Interaction**: Audit trail transparency, locked calculation previews before salary release.

### 2.8 Parent & Guardian
- **Primary Viewport**: Smartphone (iOS / Android) / WhatsApp.
- **Cognitive Goal**: Peace of mind regarding child safety, academic progress, and frictionless fee payment.
- **Key Modules Visible**:
  - Live School Bus tracking with real-time GPS and proximity alerts.
  - Digital school diary and daily learning highlights.
  - Outstanding fee card with one-click UPI checkout (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm).
  - Digital report cards with teacher audio notes or comments.
- **Tone & Interaction**: Warm, reassuring, multilingual, zero educational jargon, WhatsApp push integration.

### 2.9 Student
- **Primary Viewport**: Mobile / Laptop / Tablet.
- **Cognitive Goal**: Organized daily timetable, homework clarity, peer collaborative learning.
- **Key Modules Visible**:
  - Today's Timetable and classroom locations.
  - Homework deadlines with direct document/photo upload.
  - Examination syllabus, revision guides, and past performance analytics.
- **Tone & Interaction**: Engaging, gamified learning badges, clean dark/light themes.

### 2.10 Franchise Owner & Investor
- **Primary Viewport**: Laptop / Mobile.
- **Cognitive Goal**: Return on Investment (ROI), brand reputation, operational compliance.
- **Key Modules Visible**:
  - Campus P&L: Fee collection velocity, student acquisition costs.
  - Enrollment trends vs. school capacity.
  - Royalty calculation, invoices payable to Vedic Tree HQ.
- **Tone & Interaction**: Executive financial metrics, clean analytical charts, compliance scorecards.

### 2.11 Partner (Transport / Food / Books / Uniform Vendors)
- **Primary Viewport**: Mobile / Tablet.
- **Cognitive Goal**: Service delivery verification and fulfillment.
- **Key Modules Visible**:
  - Transport vendor: Fleet maintenance, driver roster, fuel logs.
  - Uniform/book vendor: Student inventory requirements, order fulfillment status.
- **Tone & Interaction**: Simple task checklists, barcode scanners, delivery confirmations.
